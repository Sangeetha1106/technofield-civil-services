Flooring Process

Industrial epoxy & polyurethane flooring systems

OUR TECHNICAL APPROACH

Industrial Flooring. Engineered for Performance.

We execute industrial epoxy and polyurethane flooring applications with an emphasis on proper substrate preparation, moisture testing, suitable priming and functional slip resistance.

Heavy Duty · Chemical & Abrasion Resistant
SUBSTRATE-DRIVEN ENGINEERING

Technical Precision From Base to Finish

A high-performance floor is decided long before the topcoat is applied. We treat the concrete substrate as an engineered foundation, controlling moisture vapour emissions, mechanically profiling surface porosity, and curing systems to exact chemical specifications.

Substrate Soundness Diamond grinding & shot blasting eliminate laitance.
Moisture Control Digital assessment prevents osmotic blister failures.
Functional Resilience High compressive strength suited for forklift traffic.
Seamless Hygiene Non-porous surfaces resistant to chemical spillages.
EXECUTION TIMELINE

Six-Step Flooring Execution Process

A disciplined, sequence-governed procedure applied on every industrial project.

01

STEP 01 – SUBSTRATE PREPARATION

Prepare the existing concrete surface to ensure it is suitable for the flooring system.

02

STEP 02 – SHOT BLASTING

Prepare the concrete substrate through appropriate mechanical surface preparation.

03

STEP 03 – MOISTURE TESTING

Assess substrate moisture conditions before proceeding with flooring application.

04

STEP 04 – PRIMING

Apply the appropriate primer system to the prepared substrate.

05

STEP 05 – FLOORING APPLICATION

Execute the selected industrial epoxy or polyurethane flooring system.

06

STEP 06 – FUNCTIONAL SLIP RESISTANCE

Consider suitable slip-resistance requirements based on the intended application.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Technical & Execution Inquiries

Answers to common questions regarding site inspection coordination, execution timelines, material systems and application procedures.

Ask on WhatsApp
Which areas of a building can you waterproof?

We handle basements, terraces, sunken slabs, toilets and wet areas, UG sumps and balconies, using systems suited to each location.

Do you visit the site before quoting?

Yes. We coordinate an on-site inspection so the recommended system and scope match the real condition of your structure.

Can industrial work be done at night or during shutdowns?

Yes. We plan industrial bay, night-shift and shutdown job schedules so your operations are disturbed as little as possible.

What goes into an industrial epoxy or PU floor?

Substrate preparation, shot blasting, moisture testing, priming, system application and a functional slip-resistant finish.

Which materials do you use?

We work with established systems such as Fosroc, Dr. Fixit, Tremco, Flowcrete, Weber, STP and Berger, selected after inspection.
